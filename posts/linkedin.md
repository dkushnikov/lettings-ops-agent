Most of an operations team's day is not routine work. It is exceptions: the email that contradicts the record, the contractor who did not show, the repair that costs more than the landlord allowed, the tenant who mentions a cold flat and a baby in one sentence.

At the Real-World Agents Hackathon ({team}) we built {one_line_what}.

Two ideas drove it. First, the agent acts only within authority it can cite: a clause, a landlord instruction, a statutory duty with its status. Everything else becomes a question to the right person. Second, every exception is handed over as a 90-second decision: the question, five facts with sources, the gap, the options, a default if nobody answers, and how to undo what was done. {distinctive}

It is open source under Apache 2.0, with the unseen-case results and traces in the repo: {repo_url}

Thanks to Dwelly, Anthropic, ElevenLabs, General Catalyst and EQT for the environment and the day.
